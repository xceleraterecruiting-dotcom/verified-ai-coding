# Non-goals

## Non-goals

Deliberately excluded from this plan (some may be later phases; none are forgotten thoughts):

- N1: Building or redesigning recruiting profiles themselves — they exist; this plan only adds a
  badge section to them (Slice 11).
- N2: Inventory/SKU management UI — the store sells gear "we already stock"; creating, pricing,
  or restocking SKUs stays wherever it lives today.
- N3: Self-service refunds, cancellations, or returns in the store UI. Refund **policy** must
  still be answered (OQ-04) because charge-on-order makes failure cases money-bearing, but
  building a customer-facing refund flow is out of scope; staff handle exceptions manually.
- N4: Shipping or delivery — fulfillment is exclusively hand-over at practice.
- N5: Direct/private messaging between users; the board is the only communication surface.
- N6: Parents or athletes creating top-level posts — coaches post, others comment (per spec).
- N7: Badge types beyond attendance streaks (no skill badges, manual awards, or coach-granted
  badges).
- N8: A full moderation dashboard or appeals workflow — reporting creates a moderation record and
  notifies coaches; anything richer is deferred.
- N9: Attendance **capture** — attendance records exist in the current system; this plan only
  reads them (badges, CSV export). RSVP is intent, not attendance.
- N10: Push notifications or email channels for reminders — spec asks for a text; other channels
  are deferred.
- N11: Video transcoding, streaming optimization, or in-browser film review tooling — file
  storage and authorized download only.
- N12: A formal COPPA/child-privacy compliance audit. The plan enforces the spec's under-13 rule
  and surfaces consent questions (OQ-01, OQ-02, OQ-03), but legal compliance review is the
  user's, not this plan's.
- N13: Scheduled or emailed attendance exports — CSV export is on-demand only (A6).
- N14: Payment of fees, dues, or anything other than the gear store.
- N15: Cross-cohort sharing or any "global" hub content — explicitly contrary to R18.
