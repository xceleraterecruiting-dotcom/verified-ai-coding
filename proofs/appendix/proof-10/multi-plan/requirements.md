# Requirements

## Requirements

Traceability: spec text quoted in parentheses is from the verbatim spec in `spec-intake.md`.

- R1: Each cohort gets a team hub — a shared space visible to that cohort's parents, athletes,
  and coaches ("a 'team hub' for each cohort. Parents and athletes get a shared space").
- R2: Coaches can create message-board posts in their cohort's hub ("a message board (coaches
  post...").
- R3: Parents can comment on message-board posts ("parents can comment").
- R4: Every comment passes a profanity filter before it becomes visible ("comments need a
  profanity filter").
- R5: Every visible comment carries a report button; reporting creates a moderation record
  ("...and a report button").
- R6: Athletes under 13 cannot author comments; their parents comment on their behalf ("Kids
  under 13 can't post comments themselves (parents only)").
- R7: Coaches can upload files (practice plans, film clips) to their cohort's file area ("a file
  area for practice plans and film clips (coaches upload...").
- R8: Uploads larger than 100MB are rejected ("100MB max").
- R9: Files are visible/downloadable only to members of the owning cohort ("only that cohort can
  see them").
- R10: Each session has an RSVP; cohort coaches see the resulting headcount ("RSVP for each
  session so coaches know headcount").
- R11: An automatic reminder text (SMS) is sent the morning of each session ("with an automatic
  reminder text the morning of").
- R12: Parents can buy already-stocked gear (shirts/shorts) in a small store ("a small store
  where parents can buy extra gear (shirts/shorts we already stock)").
- R13: Store purchases are paid by card, charged at order time ("with card payment — charge on
  order").
- R14: Orders are fulfilled in person at practice; staff can mark an order fulfilled ("we fulfill
  at practice").
- R15: Athletes earn badges for attendance streaks ("athletes earn badges for attendance
  streaks").
- R16: Earned badges are displayed on the athlete's public recruiting profile ("badges show on
  their public recruiting profile") — display is gated on OQ-03 (guardian consent for minors).
- R17: Coaches can export attendance as CSV ("the coaches want CSV export of attendance").
- R18: Every hub capability is scoped to cohort membership; no user can read or write another
  cohort's content ("All of it should respect who's in which cohort — nobody sees another
  cohort's stuff").
