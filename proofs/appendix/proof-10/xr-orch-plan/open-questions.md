# Open questions

## Open questions

- OQ-1 [severity: high] [status: open] What is the stable identity of a verified offer for dedupe purposes, and what is the dedupe lifecycle? Specifically: (a) does the feed provide a stable unique offer ID that survives re-delivery, or must we derive a composite key (athlete + offering college + …)? (b) does "duplicates of drafts it already made" mean the job must never re-draft an offer it has EVER drafted — including after you reject or delete the draft — or should rejection/deletion make the offer eligible again? The answer defines INV-2's predicate and the ledger schema in Slice 1; building on the wrong key either floods the queue or permanently suppresses legitimate drafts.
- OQ-2 [severity: high] [status: open] When an already-drafted offer is re-delivered with changed facts (e.g. the feed corrects a wrong date or school), what should happen: ignore (current draft stands), create a new draft, or flag/annotate the existing draft for your attention? The spec says feeds are messy, so corrections will happen; the answer changes INV-2 (is dedupe keyed on offer identity alone, or identity + content version?) and possibly adds an invariant about stale drafts of corrected facts reaching review.
- OQ-3 [severity: medium] [status: open] One draft per offer, or one draft per athlete-burst? "a draft post for each one" says per-offer, but "the offer(s) it came from" admits grouping a kid's burst of offers into one post. Adopted default: per-offer (A2/IN-2), with a many-to-many trace schema so grouping later is additive.
- OQ-4 [severity: medium] [status: open] Partial-but-truthful dates (e.g. month/year known, day missing): render at the known precision ("offered in March 2026"), omit the date from the draft, or fail the record? Adopted default (A3/IN-6): truthfully-representable partial dates render at known precision; unparseable/contradictory dates fail the record and appear in run history; a date is never guessed (INV-7).
- OQ-5 [severity: medium] [status: open] What does "recent" mean operationally — does "lands in our database" key on created-at or verified-at, and what lookback window should the eligibility query use? Adopted default: verified-at within a window comfortably larger than the run cadence (correctness carried by the dedupe ledger per IN-3, so the window is an efficiency knob only).
- OQ-6 [severity: medium] [status: open] Exact sanitization contract for stored errors: is the proposed allowlist (failure category + human-readable message + offer reference + internal correlation ID, never stack traces / secrets / raw payload or model dumps) the right "presentable" bar for the internal dashboard, and may the offer reference include the athlete's name or only an ID?
- OQ-7 [severity: low] [status: open] Exact run cadence ("every few minutes") and whether it must be operator-configurable without a deploy.
- OQ-8 [severity: medium] [status: open] Is there (or should there be) an opt-out/suppression list — athletes, families, or schools whose offers must not even be drafted? Current posture (A8): public facts only + human review gate suffices, so this stays medium; if the answer is "yes, suppression must gate draft creation", it becomes a new L2/L3 invariant and the risk classification escalates (see risk-map.md).
