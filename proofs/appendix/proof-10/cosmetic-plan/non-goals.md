# Non-goals

## Non-goals

- No redesign or restructuring of the landing page — "a paint job, not a rebuild".
- No changes to sections, navigation, links, or forms (explicitly excluded by the spec).
- No copy changes other than the hero headline.
- No new testimonial content, testimonial management, or CMS work — only auto-rotation of the
  existing cards.
- No analytics, A/B testing, or measurement of the refresh.
- No performance, SEO, or accessibility overhaul beyond not regressing what exists (the one
  surfaced constraint, reduced-motion handling for the carousel, is scoped inside Slice 2).
- No image pipeline / optimization work beyond dropping in the supplied team-shot asset.
